Build a **premium, high-end website** for **Ultimate Fitness And Tanning – Chandler, Texas**.

**Business:** Locally owned 24-hour gym & tanning facility. Largest facility and lowest prices. Serves everyone from bodybuilders, powerlifters and bikini competitors to state-champion athletes and everyday fitness enthusiasts.

### Design Direction

Create a **bold, premium, powerful fitness website** — NOT a generic gym template or AI-looking website. Use a dark cinematic visual direction, aggressive typography, strong contrast, premium gradients, subtle glow effects, smooth scrolling and polished motion animations. The first impression must feel like a serious, established training facility.

### Sections

1. **Hero** — extremely strong first impression with bold headline, powerful CTA, 24/7 messaging and premium animated visual treatment. **Do not use stock video.**
2. **About / Facility** — communicate locally owned, 24-hour access, largest facility and affordable pricing.
3. **Training** — showcase strength training, bodybuilding, athletic performance, bikini competition preparation, personal training and everyday fitness.
4. **Why Ultimate Fitness** — premium feature/stat section highlighting 24/7 access, facility size, community and value.
5. **Facebook Reels** — create a visually impressive **4-video section**. I will provide 4 real Facebook Reel videos later. **Leave all 4 video areas empty/placeholders and DO NOT generate, download or use any stock videos.**
6. **Testimonials / Social Proof** — premium modern testimonial/review section. Do not invent reviews; leave editable content if real reviews are not provided.
7. **Final CTA** — strong “Start Your Fitness Journey” conversion section with call/contact CTA.
8. **Footer** — address, phone, email, hours, Facebook and clean navigation.

Make everything **fully responsive**, especially mobile. Keep the implementation focused on the actual website and avoid unnecessary explanations or extra pages.
